## How I write emails

### Tone
[Describe your voice — e.g., "Direct and conversational. I don't use buzzwords. I write like I talk."]

### What I always do
- Start with something specific about them (not generic)
- Keep it under 100 words for cold outreach
- One ask per email — never two CTAs
- Use short sentences. No walls of text.

### What I never say
- "I hope this finds you well"
- "Just wanted to touch base"
- "Circle back"
- "Synergy" / "leverage" / "unlock value"
