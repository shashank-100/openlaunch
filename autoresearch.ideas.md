## Untested Ideas

### Email Writing Tactics
- [ ] Add social proof ("helping 50+ B2B companies...")
- [ ] Use FOMO ("limited spots this quarter")
- [ ] Lead with a question instead of statement
- [ ] Reference recent company news in first line
- [ ] Use emoji sparingly for visual breaks
- [ ] A/B test formal vs casual tone
- [ ] Include 1-line PS with alternative CTA

### Personalization
- [ ] Mention prospect's LinkedIn post/comment
- [ ] Reference mutual connection (if exists)
- [ ] Acknowledge their specific role responsibilities
- [ ] Tie product benefit to their department goals

### Timing Optimizations
- [ ] Send on Tuesday-Thursday only (avoid Mon/Fri)
- [ ] Optimize send time to recipient's timezone
- [ ] Test morning (9-11am) vs afternoon (2-4pm)
- [ ] Reduce follow-up frequency to Day 4, 10 only

### Targeting Improvements
- [ ] Only contact companies with 50-500 employees
- [ ] Focus on recently funded companies only
- [ ] Prioritize companies hiring in relevant roles
- [ ] Filter out companies with competing solutions
